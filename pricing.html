Common Questions
How long does implementation take?

Typical implementation is 30 days, deployed in three phases: Connect & Configure (Week 1–2), Communication Intelligence (Week 2–3), and Full Operational Intelligence (Week 3–4). No disruption to guest experience during rollout.

Do I need to replace my existing PMS or POS?

No. Lodgient sits on top of your existing systems — including Maestro, Opera, Silverware, Simphony, and others. We integrate via API. You don't rip and replace anything.

Can I start with just RoomService AI?

Absolutely. Most hotels start with RoomService AI and expand into additional modules as they see the operational lift. The platform is modular by design.

Is there a minimum contract period?

We offer flexible terms. Contact our team for specific details based on your property type and requirements.

Do you support multi-property deployments?

Yes. Lodgient Enterprise is built specifically for multi-property groups with centralised governance, cross-property benchmarking, and portfolio-level visibility.

The Reality

Your hotel runs on systems that don't talk to each other.

→ A booking arrives with no deposit captured. Nobody flags it until checkout — or never.

→ A guest calls at 11pm about a broken radiator. The message is scribbled on paper. By morning, it's lost.

→ Housekeeping finishes Room 214, but Front Desk doesn't know for another 20 minutes. The guest waits.

→ Your best upsell window — the guest calling about dining — goes to voicemail because the team is handling check-ins.

→ A VIP booking has dietary requirements in the PMS. The kitchen never sees them. The welcome dinner is wrong.

These aren't edge cases. They happen every day.

Your PMS holds reservations. Your POS processes orders. Your phone system takes calls. Your housekeeping team uses radios or WhatsApp. Your night manager leaves a handover note.

None of these systems are connected. None enforce follow-through. None tell you when something falls through the cracks.

The issue isn't your people. It's the absence of an operational layer that connects them.

Lodgient is that layer.

How It Works

Five operational layers. One connected property.

01Command

Operational Command Centre

What your team sees: A single operational inbox for every department.
  • Requests routed to the correct department instantly
  • Every task tracked to completion with timestamps
  • Shift handovers captured digitally
  • Escalation rules trigger automatically
  • Full audit trail for management

A guest requests extra towels and a late checkout. The towel request goes to Housekeeping. The late checkout goes to Front Desk. Both are tracked. If Housekeeping hasn't confirmed in 15 minutes, the Duty Manager is notified. The guest never calls twice.

02Revenue

Revenue Protection

What your team sees: A validation layer that catches errors before they become losses.
  • Bookings checked against deposit policies automatically
  • Rate integrity and inclusion accuracy validated
  • Flagged exceptions surfaced before arrival
  • Missed call revenue recovered via Voice Intelligence
  • Structured reporting on revenue at risk

A group booking for 12 rooms: negotiated rate, breakfast included, deposit required. Lodgient validates each element. If the deposit is missing, it flags the booking to your revenue manager 48 hours before arrival — not at checkout.

03Voice

Communication Intelligence

What your team sees: Every guest interaction captured, transcribed, and actioned.
  • AI voice handling — after-hours, overflow, or full-time
  • Every call transcribed, logged, and searchable
  • Service requests auto-created as tasks
  • Intelligent routing to the right department
  • No more scribbled messages or lost voicemails

Why Now

Your hotel tech was built to store records. Not to run operations.

Most hotels operate on technology designed 10–20 years ago. Maestro, Silverware, Opera, and dozens of others were built as systems of record. They were never designed to:

→ Coordinate work across departments in real time
→ Enforce that a task was completed, not just assigned
→ Capture and structure guest communications
→ Flag revenue leakage before it occurs
→ Give management operational visibility without making phone calls

Lodgient doesn't ask you to replace these systems. It sits on top of them — integrating with your existing PMS and POS — and adds the operational intelligence layer they were never built to provide. We work with what you already have. You don't rip and replace. You upgrade how your hotel actually operates.
